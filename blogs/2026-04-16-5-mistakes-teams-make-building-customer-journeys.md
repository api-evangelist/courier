Most journey problems don't announce themselves. They show up as rising unsubscribe rates, softer engagement, and a sense that messaging used to work better. This post breaks down five mistakes teams repeat when building customer journeys: shipping too many without coordination, building journeys with no exit criteria, treating channels as interchangeable, letting the org chart dictate the flow, and using time delays where event triggers belong.
